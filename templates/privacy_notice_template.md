## Separate acknowledgement and optional permissions

Present these as separate, initially unchecked controls alongside the notice link. They are outside S01–S19.

- [ ] I acknowledge that I have read this privacy notice.
- [ ] I give [Organisation Name] permission to contact me about discussing my business improvements. (Optional)
- [ ] I give [Organisation Name] permission to send me marketing communications described here: [Actual marketing scope, channels and unsubscribe process]. (Optional)

Interest in a discussion is not contact permission. Privacy acknowledgement is not marketing permission. Record explicit choices as `true`/`false`, unknown choices as blank, and capture the notice version and timestamp where possible. Store them in the restricted contacts/consent dataset.
